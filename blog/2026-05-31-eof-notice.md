title: End of Life Notice
---

Starting from 1 June 2026, the following releases have reached end of life
and no longer receive maintenance, security patches, or compatibility support:

- wasmd `v0.54.x`
- wasmvm `v2.2.x`
- cosmwasm-\* `v2.2.x`

This is aligned with EOL notices for
[Cosmos SDK](https://docs.cosmos.network/sdk/latest/release-family#end-of-life-notices) `v0.50.x`.
